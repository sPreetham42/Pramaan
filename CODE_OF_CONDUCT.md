# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

Everyone in the PRAMAAN team — students, mentors, and contributors — is
treated with respect. We are building this project together, and a
supportive environment matters as much as working code.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, even when you disagree
- Assume good intent; ask questions before assuming the worst
- Give and receive feedback kindly
- Help teammates grow; credit others for their work
- Keep discussions focused on the project

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Personal attacks, trolling, or demeaning comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any behavior that makes a teammate uncomfortable contributing

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, tell the repository
owner / team lead privately — in person, in the team chat, or by direct
message. Reports are kept confidential and handled promptly and fairly.
Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply
immediately.

## Scope

This applies to all PRAMAAN spaces: GitHub (issues, PRs, discussions),
team chat, and any in-person or virtual team events.
